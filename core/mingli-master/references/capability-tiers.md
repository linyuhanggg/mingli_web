# Executable Capability Tiers

Corpus coverage, deterministic calculation, and directional prediction are
three different capabilities. A complete ancient-book pack does not by itself
authorize a personal prediction. `scripts/route_capabilities.py` is the runtime
source of truth; this document explains its release policy.

## Hard Rules

1. D2-ready means the source pack can be retrieved and cited. It does not mean
   the corresponding chart can be calculated or that its rules form an
   executable predictor.
2. A Tier-B or Tier-C route cannot be promoted by persuasive prose. The
   interpretation finalizer rejects a directional verdict mechanically.
3. Cross-system corroboration requires at least two valid directional artifacts
   for the same question and dimension. One directional artifact plus one
   underdetermined artifact is `single_system_only`, not agreement.
4. Multiple books in one lineage are correlated evidence. Use
   `references/inference/source-lineages-v1.json`; book count is never vote count.
5. A model's confidence cannot exceed the direct tool's `confidence_ceiling`.
   High confidence additionally needs two independent mechanism groups and two
   registered source lineages.
6. The question itself also has a ceiling. Exact year, amount, location, and
   closed candidate-event selection default to low confidence unless a future
   promoted rule tool directly discriminates that requested precision. A broad
   tendency rule cannot be promoted into an exact event by fluent prose.
7. There is no category taboo. A difficult, private, financial, health, or
   relationship question may be answered directly when the fact layer exists.
   But exact state/event identity is an experimental hypothesis, not a fact.
8. Tier A means the fact and inference pipeline is executable and guarded. It
   does not mean empirical predictive accuracy has been established. The v16
   blind result is recorded in `references/regression/v16-architecture-validation.md`.

## Promotion Gate

A route moves to Tier A only after all of the following exist:

1. A version-pinned deterministic adapter with calendar/profile trace.
2. Structural and semantic fact validation with conflict fixtures.
3. An executable rule compiler whose activations bind exact facts, rules,
   applicability conditions, stop conditions, and confidence ceilings.
4. Answer-isolated development tests plus an untouched held-out set whose
   advertised prediction scope beats its preregistered baseline with adequate
   coverage and uncertainty reporting.
5. A production wrapper that freezes facts, evidence, inference, interpretation,
   and final public text by digest.
6. Natural-language follow-up replays proving that the chart or lesson is reused
   and the user is not asked to repeat bound information.

Until all six pass, the route may provide chart facts, source research, or a
clearly underdetermined reading, but it must not imitate a calculated verdict.
