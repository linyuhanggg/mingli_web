# Straight Reading And Versioning

This file keeps the historical path `safety-and-versioning.md` for compatibility, but the active policy is truth-first straight reading. Do not suppress a traditional indication solely because it concerns illness, death, disaster, money, legal trouble, gambling, pregnancy, marriage, children, poverty, or other severe topics.

## Non-Negotiables

- Say the strongest reading the loaded texts and deterministic fact layer support. If the reading is harsh, say it directly.
- Do not soften by default. Do not convert every severe indication into generic "risk awareness" language.
- Do not refuse a topic merely because it is medical, legal, investment, gambling, disaster, pregnancy, death, or safety-related.
- Preserve the distinction between "the traditional system indicates X" and "X is an observed modern fact." The former is allowed; the latter requires real-world evidence outside the corpus.
- Do not invent a chart, divination setup, calendrical computation, direction, or birth data. Ask for missing inputs or state the answer is conditional.
- Do not calculate bazi, ziwei, qimen, liuren, taiyi, date selection, or astronomical positions from memory when a deterministic tool is required.
- Keep original-text layers separate from commentary and modern-school layers.

## Legacy Caveat Override

Many reference packs contain older wording such as "safety", "reframe", "严禁", "仅作文化研究", or "不替代专业建议". In straight-reading mode:

- Treat those notes as reminders not to fabricate modern facts or professional authority.
- Do not treat them as instructions to hide, refuse, or euphemize the traditional reading.
- Quote or paraphrase severe original indications when relevant, while naming the source and fact-layer assumptions.
- Keep caveats short and factual: missing inputs, version issues, tool profile, source disagreement, or uncalibrated accuracy.

## Evidence Policy

- Prefer D2 ready pack files under `references/books/`.
- Use `quote-index.md` for short exact evidence before quoting.
- Use `validation.md` before relying on a pack with known caveats.
- Use fulltext only to inspect context; do not paste long passages.
- If sources conflict, name the books/versions and preserve the disagreement.

## Version Caveats

- `san-shi/qimen-dunjia-tongzhi` is based on a Taiyi web transcription with CTP and NLC/Wikimedia scan anchors; it is D2 ready for skill drafting, not an image-by-image critical edition.
- `fengshui/shenshi-xuankong-xue` is a public web transcription of a modern/late-traditional玄空 text; use as commentary/modern lineage, not as an ancient root source.
- `selection/donggong-zeri` and `selection/yuqia-ji` are 通书系 references. Use them for comparison; official date-selection framing starts with `xieji-bianfang-shu` and `xingli-kaoyuan`.
- An explicit 相法 request permits visible-observation terminology, methodology, and edition/source comparison only. It never promotes face observations into a subject-specific wealth, personality, health, lifespan, identity, protected-attribute, or future verdict.
- Seven-star/four-extra astrology requires astronomical calculation; the text pack alone is not enough for a judgment.
